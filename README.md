# Sourcing a Windlass Tourniquet: OEM Manufacturing Realities Beyond the Standard Brands

When developing or procuring trauma kits for military, law enforcement, or industrial first aid, product designers often evaluate established tourniquet designs such as the North American Rescue CAT Gen 7 and the TacMed Solutions SOFTT-W Gen 5. These legacy devices utilize specific windlass mechanisms, nylon webbing widths, and single- or double-routing buckle configurations established through decades of tactical field evolution. However, for medical device brands seeking to manufacture their own hardware, analyzing these benchmarks is merely the starting point; the critical decision lies in selecting an original equipment manufacturer (OEM) capable of reproducing precise specifications reliably at scale.

### Comparing Design Benchmarks and Manufacturing Realities

The CAT Gen 7 features a rigid plastic windlass rod, a free-moving internal band for true circumferential pressure, and a single-routing buckle system designed to simplify application under stress. The SOFTT-W Gen 5 employs a wider high-strength webbing and a robust aluminum windlass rod secured by dual-locking triangles. While both designs define functional performance parameters in the field, neither brand operates as an open-market manufacturing partner for third-party device developers. Sourcing equivalent mechanical performance requires partnering with an industrial facility equipped for precision molding, heavy-duty webbing assembly, and strict tension testing.
